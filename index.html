02WHY IT MATTERS

Education should open a door—not ask a student to wait outside it.

AWF’s work is rooted in a simple belief: access to quality education changes what a young person can imagine for themselves. The repository extends that belief into the digital world, giving students a practical way to learn GitHub, share work, and see themselves as makers.
